# 🪨 SOLID

**SOLID principles are fundamental guidelines that help developers design efficient object-oriented software.**

In the year 2000, an acclaimed software engineer _Robert C. Martin aka Uncle Bob_ published a paper titled [“Design Principles and Design 
Patterns”](https://staff.cs.utu.fi/~jounsmed/doos_06/material/DesignPrinciplesAndPatterns.pdf) in which he introduced the SOLID principles. They fundamentally changed the way developers approach object-oriented design, emphasizing the importance of modularity, maintainability, and extensibility.
